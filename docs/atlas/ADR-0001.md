# UHE ATLAS — Architecture Decision Record 0001

Status: PROPOSED / discovery branch; not a production release.

## Mission
Convert real tender documents into evidence-linked executable requirements, mathematically qualify opportunities, derive parametric geometry and bills of quantities, and compile coordinated technical, financial and design deliverables with auditable release gates.

## Non-negotiable invariants
1. Every extracted requirement retains source file, page/location, exact source excerpt, extraction confidence and review status.
2. Mandatory eligibility failures block automatic bid recommendations; unknown eligibility cannot silently pass.
3. Geometry uses a single coordinate system, explicit units, verified venue constraints and deterministic calculations. No invented venue dimensions.
4. Technical, financial and design outputs derive from the same typed project objects and revision.
5. Every rate has provenance, currency, unit, effective date and estimate/quotation status. Supplier costs and margins never appear in client outputs.
6. No AI-generated visual imagery in UHE proposals without project-specific permission. Use controlled CAD/vector/3D geometry.
7. 3D is required only when the RFP mandates it or evaluation strategy justifies it; mandatory design deliverables cannot be waived by automation.
8. Legal declarations, commercial approval, signatures and submission require authorized human approval.
9. Client documents use the exact prescribed filenames and pass visual, arithmetic, compliance and metadata checks before release.
10. All models, prompts, datasets, benchmarks and document templates are versioned; confidential tender content must not be committed to public repositories.

## Initial service boundaries
- intake: document parsing, provenance and evidence ledger
- requirements: typed requirement graph and compliance state
- qualification: hard gates, weighted opportunity score and calibrated bid model
- geometry: parametric 2D constraints and optional 3D derived from the same master
- commercial: unit-aware BOQ, selling rates, VAT and reconciliation
- compiler: verified templates and document exports
- verification: deterministic tests, visual QA and human release controls

## First acceptance test
Given a complete authorized RFP, the system must output a source-linked requirement matrix with explicit unknowns, a reproducible bid/no-bid calculation, a unit-consistent geometry/BOQ fixture where applicable, and a machine-readable QA report. No fabricated claims or release status.
